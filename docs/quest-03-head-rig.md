# Quest 03 first-person head rig

Stage 03 replaces the Stage 02 tabletop ring with a first-person robot-eye view in fully virtual OpenXR on top of merged PR #2 (`docs/quest-02-arena-menu.md`). It is not yet worn on a headset; Stage 01, Stage 02, and Stage 03 headset gates remain **pending**.

## Coordinate spaces

- **S0 (Sim):** `ArenaController` simulation metres (`ARENA_RULES`, `ARENA_LAYOUT`), unchanged. Phone AR and combat math are untouched.
- **S1 (Ring-local tabletop):** `fighterY = ARENA_LAYOUT.floorHeight + ARENA_LAYOUT.footOffset`. The player eye anchor `eyeRingLocal(anchor)` places `ROBOT_EYE_MODEL = [0, 1.1271, 0.2028]` (from `eye_l`/`eye_r` rest midpoint in both fighter GLBs) in the frozen player fighter frame, **ignoring `lift`** so hops/hits never move the camera.
- **S2 (VR metres):** `S1 * QUEST_WORLD_SCALE` (`QUEST_WORLD_SCALE = 16`, a reversible tuning constant). At `S = 16`, robot height is `~1.92 m`, eye height above the fighting floor is `~1.355 m`, spawn gap is `3.04 m`, and default eye-to-opponent center distance is `~2.80 m`.
- **S3 (OpenXR `LOCAL`):** Untouched native 6DoF scene root (`VROSceneRendererOpenXR`). No `<ViroCamera>`, no `ViroGameLoop` per-frame rig updates, no head bob, no hit shake, and no native `recenterTracking`.
- **S4 (Calibration frame):** `{ origin, yaw, posture, source, entry }` in S3 (`DEFAULT_CALIBRATION = { origin: [0, 0, 0], yaw: 180, posture: 'standing', source: 'default', entry: 0 }`), using the repo `facing` yaw convention (`0 = +Z`, `+90 = +X`, `180 = -Z`).
- **S5 (Gameplay head pose):** Output-only bounded offsets (`lateral`, `vertical`, `forward`, and `/ QUEST_WORLD_SCALE` in `sim`), `yawRelative`, `clamped`, `excess`, and `outOfBounds = excess > 0.30`.

## Rig hierarchy, calibration, and comfort decisions

- **Static horizon-locked rig:** `QuestRing` wraps S1 content in a 3-node `<ViroNode>` rig (`position={rig.position}` + Y-only `rotation={[0, rig.rotationY, 0]}` -> `scale={[16, 16, 16]}` -> `position={rig.contentOffset}`). The rig updates only on explicit calibration events (`calibrateForSolo`, `recenter`, `cyclePosture`), never from per-frame head samples.
- **Own-body occlusion vs real asset lease:** `Mike_Player1_Blue_Combat_AR.glb` shares a single body mesh (`SKM_MegaMikeZ_part_0`) with eyes inside the visor, so the blue `Viro3DObject` stays mounted with `visible={false}` and retains its real `onLoadEnd`/`onError` callbacks. A stance floor ring marks the calibrated position and tints when `outOfBounds` is true. It is centered under the robot eyes (`stanceMarkerRingLocal(anchor)`), which the rig places on the calibrated head, not under the fighter's feet, which are `ROBOT_EYE_MODEL` z (`0.244 m` in VR) behind the eyes. Fighters, arena, and `QUEST_WORLD_SCALE` are unchanged.
- **Sample pipeline & lifecycle:** `ViroScene.onCameraTransformUpdate` writes validated samples (`headSampleFromViro`, rejecting non-finite/zero vectors and normalizing `forward`/`up`) into a 64-entry O(1) ring buffer; store subscribers and the scaled (`0.05`) diagnostics `ViroText` strip update at most 5 Hz, and `ReactNativeJS` logcat pose summaries log at most 1 Hz.
- **First-Solo-per-entry calibration:** Keyed to `questRuntime.getSnapshot().entries`. First `Solo` in an entry calibrates from the latest stable window (else latest valid sample, else `DEFAULT_CALIBRATION` with status `'no-samples'`). `Back -> Solo` within the same entry keeps calibration; if `Recenter` already succeeded on the menu page in that entry, `Solo` preserves it and binds `anchor`.
- **App-only Recenter & Posture:** `Recenter` requires a post-press `500 ms` window within `2000 ms` with `>= 8` valid samples, position spread `<= 0.03 m`, and horizontal-forward yaw spread `<= 5°` (median position, circular-mean yaw `atan2(fx, fz)`). Failure leaves calibration unchanged. `Posture` cycles `'standing' <-> 'seated'` and updates only `QUEST_HEAD_BOUNDS`:
  - `standing`: lateral `±0.30 m`, crouch `0.45 m`, rise `0.05 m`, forward/back `±0.20 m`
  - `seated`: lateral `±0.25 m`, crouch `0.25 m`, rise `0.05 m`, forward/back `±0.20 m`
- **Reference-frame jump recovery (raw samples only):** ViroCore v3.0.3 receives `XR_TYPE_EVENT_DATA_REFERENCE_SPACE_CHANGE_PENDING` in `VROSceneRendererOpenXR::pollEvents` but only `ALOGV`s it; nothing reaches JS. The tracker therefore looks at consecutive raw `LOCAL` samples. A step counts as a frame jump only when `dt <= 100 ms` and it moves `>= max(0.25 m, 4 m/s * dt)` or turns `>= max(45°, 900°/s * dt)` (`isReferenceDiscontinuity`). A 0.2 m lean, a fast duck, or a 400°/s head turn does not count, and nothing across a longer gap does. A candidate must be followed by 6 continuous samples (about 80 ms). A second discontinuity inside that window is a glitch: it is rejected and the detector cools down. On confirmation, `rebaseCalibration` moves origin/yaw through the same yaw-plus-translation (never pitch or roll), so the world keeps its place around the wearer and the gameplay pose is unchanged. It is skipped for the default (pre-Solo) frame, which follows `LOCAL` by design, and when Solo or Recenter recalibrated inside the confirm window. Clamp or out-of-bounds state never triggers it. The tracked camera is never driven. Sparse `[QuestHeadRig]` logs record candidate (dt, delta, raw from/to, origin/yaw), rebase, or rejection. The 1 Hz line adds raw position/yaw, calibrated origin/yaw, excess, and reset count. The menu hint `'If the system recenter moved the view, select Recenter.'` remains for jumps below the thresholds.
- **Clean compositor menu & poses:** `QuestMenu` renders via the OpenXR underlay compositor (`modules/quest-panel` + `tools/quest/viro-renderer/0002-openxr-quest-panel-layer.patch`, adapted from Medical Viewer) with a clean vector/text UI (no menu PNG or branding assets), `hdrEnabled={false}` and `bloomEnabled={false}` on `ViroXRSceneNavigator` (so Viro writes the zero-alpha `questMenuLayerMask` hole directly into the eye swapchain), trigger-only input (`questTriggerPress`/`questPointerHover`, gaze source 12 ignored), a Viro `QuestMenuFocus` hover outline (so pointing never redraws the panel), and `1.6×0.9 m` two-row layout (`QUEST_MENU_TEXT_LINES_MAX = 6`), wrapped in `menuFrame(tracker.calibration)`. Compositor layer and hit targets share one posed node. Poses (both upright, rotation `[0, 0, 0]`):
  - `menu`: `[0, -0.08, -1.5]`, `1.50 m` away, `3°` below eye level, `~56°` wide (was `[0, 0.08, -1.15]`).
  - `ring`: `[0, -0.85, -1.35]`, `1.60 m` away, `32°` down, `~61°` wide (was `[0, -0.78, -0.5]`, `0.93 m` and `57°` down). The opponent's head, torso, and bounding-box top corners stay above the panel. The lower legs (bottom `0.49–0.64 m` of the `1.92 m` robot, depending on depth) are behind it while the menu is up. That trade-off was accepted for reading comfort and is pinned by `tests/quest-arena.test.cjs`.
- **Responsive page changes:** A page change moves the pose, compositor mask, and live hit targets together on the next frame with no loading block; `QuestPanelModule` updates pixels and pose atomically via `nativeDraw`. A trigger press dropped by the stale-target guard logs `QuestMenu click <action> blocked ...` once per press. If the compositor layer fails, is unavailable, or is slow (`> 2000 ms`), Viro fallback buttons take over so controls remain usable.
